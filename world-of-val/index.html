Meet Val

More than a username.

Val is the online name of Vayumi Hanamura, a young creator who has dreamed of becoming a content creator since age nine.

At 14, that dream started becoming real. What began as making TikToks with friends grew into a personal creative journey and a growing online community.

THE FIRST VIDEO

Val's first TikTok was a Satoru Gojo quote from Jujutsu Kaisen — a small beginning that became the first page of a much bigger story.

Community

There is a lot more ahead.

There are also big equestrian dreams: continuing to pursue horses and barrel racing, with the goal of potentially reaching the NFR.
