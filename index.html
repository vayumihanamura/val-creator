01 / About

A dream that became a creator journey.

I've wanted to become a content creator since I was nine years old. At 14, I started turning that dream into something real.

Originally, TikTok was something I started with friends because we hoped we'd all become influencers together. Life changed, but I kept creating and developed my own identity as Val.

Anime, horses, dirt bikes, and other interests have always been a big part of who I am. Finding people online who shared those interests made me feel seen, and that's the kind of space I want to create for other people.
